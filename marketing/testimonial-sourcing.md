# Getting real testimonials onto the site

The site has a testimonials section built and ready (`site/index.html`, hidden
behind the `hidden` attribute until real quotes exist). This is the plan to fill it
without inventing anything.

## The unlock: TechzQuad already vouches for you in public

techzquad.com lists **Forgeline Technologies** under "Our partners", linked to
forgelinetechnologies.com, alongside Apex and Build With Romeo.

That is a third-party, publicly verifiable statement that you are a real working
partner — not a claim you made about yourself. It is the strongest asset you have
right now, and it costs nothing to use.

## Ask 1 — a partner quote from TechzQuad (do this first)

Fastest real testimonial available. Existing relationship, no cold ask, and any
prospect can verify it by loading techzquad.com and seeing Forgeline in the
partner list.

> Hey [name] — quick favour. I'm rebuilding the Forgeline site and I'd like to put
> a short quote from you on it, since we've actually worked together.
>
> 2-3 sentences on what it's like handing work to me — the kind of thing you'd tell
> another agency owner who asked whether to use me. Blunt is fine; specifics are
> better than praise ("turned around X in Y", "didn't have to check his work").
>
> Happy to draft something for you to edit if that's faster. And if you want a
> reciprocal partner link back to TechzQuad from my site, say the word — glad to.

The reciprocal link is a genuine sweetener: mutual partner links help you both, and
it makes the ask a trade rather than a favour.

## Ask 2 — end-client quotes, via warm intro

TechzQuad's client logo wall overlaps heavily with your own work grid:

| On TechzQuad's site | On your work grid |
|---|---|
| THE Student | Talk Global Study |
| Coffs | Coffs City Lockmart |
| Dream Finance Group | (case study on their site) |
| Reverse Mortgage Finance Solutions | (case study on their site) |
| Afjoe Financial Services | (case study on their site) |

If you built any of those through TechzQuad, they can broker the introduction. A
warm intro converts far better than you emailing the client directly, and it keeps
you on the right side of the relationship instead of appearing to poach.

> Would you be up for introducing me to [client] for a short testimonial? Only
> about the work I did, nothing that cuts across your relationship with them —
> and I'll send you the wording before it goes live.

Sending them the wording first is what makes this safe to ask.

## Ask 3 — the clients that are yours directly

For anything you were hired for directly, ask the client themselves:

> Would you mind writing 2-3 sentences on what it was like working with me, and
> what the site did for the business? Happy to draft something for you to edit if
> that's easier.

Offering the draft roughly doubles the reply rate. A quote naming a number beats
one saying you were nice to work with — ask what changed after launch: enquiries,
bookings, load time, hours saved.

## Do NOT reuse TechzQuad's two testimonials

Their site carries these:

- Hon. Rodel Evidente, Chairman, Barangay Burgos, Cadiz City — on the Barangay
  Information System
- Hon. Dante Sabillo, Chairman, Brgy. Tabun-ac, Toboso — "The team is very
  responsive..."

Both are attributed to TechzQuad and to *their team*, on their product. Moving them
onto the Forgeline site would be misattribution — and it's the kind of thing that
detonates a partner relationship if spotted.

If you personally worked on BIS, the correct move is to ask TechzQuad to go back to
those same officials for a **fresh** quote about your part specifically.

## Loose end: Municipality of Toboso

`site/assets/projects/municipality-of-toboso.jpg` is sitting in the repo unused —
it's the only project screenshot not on the page. TechzQuad features Toboso as
their project ("Official website of Toboso, a coastal municipality in the province
of Negros Occidental").

Decide which it is:

- **You built it** → add it as an 18th work card, and bump the counts (hero note,
  work standfirst, and the "All 17" filter label) from 17 to 18.
- **You didn't** → delete the image so it can't get published by accident.

## Friendly heads-up worth sending TechzQuad

Their stats band claims "1K+ projects delivered" and "500+ happy clients", but the
installation counters on the featured projects render as **"0+ installations"** —
almost certainly a counter that isn't receiving its value. Sitting right next to
real numbers, it reads as broken.

Flagging that is free goodwill, it's exactly the service you sell, and it pairs
naturally with the testimonial ask.
